# Welcome to BuildBin #1

Hey maker 👋

Thanks for being here at the very start. BuildBin is a simple idea: tell us your board and your goal, and we'll match you with real, proven projects, along with the parts, tools, and a rough idea of cost and time.

Each issue, we'll hand-pick a few builds worth your bench time. Here's the first batch, picked to cover a range of skill levels:

### 1. Pi-hole network ad blocker · *Beginner · ~1–2 hrs · approx. $35–80*
Got a Raspberry Pi in a drawer somewhere? Turn it into a DNS filter that blocks ads for every device on your network.
**You'll need:** Raspberry Pi, a 16GB+ microSD card, a 5V power supply, and an Ethernet cable.

### 2. WLED addressable LED controller · *Beginner · ~1–2 hrs · approx. $20–50*
An ESP32 or D1 mini plus a WS2812B strip gets you app-controlled lighting effects over Wi-Fi. It's a great first soldering project.
**Pro tip:** use a level shifter and a 1000µF cap across the supply, and your strip will thank you.

### 3. CO2 monitor with SCD40 · *Beginner · ~1–2 hrs · approx. $40–70*
A Sensirion SCD40 on an ESP32 or Pico W tells you when it's time to crack a window. It's surprisingly eye-opening in a home office.

### 4. Tiny Whoop indoor micro drone · *Intermediate · ~2–4 hrs · approx. $100–180*
A ducted 1S quad you can fly around the living room. Building one teaches you Betaflight basics without risking a 5-inch crash.
**You'll need:** a 65–75mm frame, a 1S AIO flight controller with ELRS, 0702/0802 motors, a nano FPV camera, and 1S LiPos.

### 5. Self-balancing robot · *Advanced · ~6–10 hrs · approx. $50–90*
An MPU6050, two motors, and a PID loop make a two-wheeled robot that balances itself. Tuning it is frustrating at first, and you'll love it once it works.
