Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

BuildBin (buildbin.dev) is a small directory of maker projects. We collect as little as we can. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get it deleted.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you subscribe to the weekly build list. That is the only personal information we ask for. We use it to send the newsletter (and a one-time welcome email) and nothing else. We don't sell or rent it, and we don't share it with advertisers or sponsors.
  • Email delivery: subscriber emails are stored and sent through our email provider, Resend, which processes them on our behalf.
  • Sponsors: if you buy a sponsorship, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email and purchase details, but never your full card number.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and reports only aggregated numbers (like page visits, referrers and country). We can't identify you from it.

Cookies and affiliate links

BuildBin itself doesn't set tracking cookies. The only thing we save in your browser is a small local setting that remembers if you closed a seasonal banner, and it never leaves your device. Parts links go to Amazon, and as an Amazon Associate BuildBin earns from qualifying purchases. When you click an Amazon link, Amazon may set its own cookies to credit the referral, under Amazon's privacy notice. Other sites we link to (build guides, sponsors) have their own policies.

Unsubscribing and deleting your data

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe option: reply “unsubscribe” or email hello@buildbin.dev. To see, correct or delete the email address we hold for you, email the same address and we'll handle it within 30 days. We keep your email only while you're subscribed.

Children

BuildBin is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Our kids projects are meant for parents and teachers to build with children, and the newsletter is for ages 13+ and parents. If you think a child under 13 has subscribed, email hello@buildbin.dev and we'll delete the address.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Email hello@buildbin.dev.
